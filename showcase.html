Business partnership

A team behind the work.

For selected projects, Skill Cartel may provide a managed-business arrangement: we help operate and grow the agreed online business in exchange for a share of sales.

Performance-based collaboration

The proposed model is a 40% share per sale for Skill Cartel, subject to project approval and a written agreement defining responsibilities, revenue calculations, costs, refunds, chargebacks and payment schedules.

Discuss a Partnership
Partnerships are assessed individually. No sales, income or business outcome is guaranteed. Final terms must be agreed in a signed contract before work begins.
